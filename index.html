What is CPS Test?

CPS (Clicks Per Second) test is a tool designed to measure how fast you can click your mouse or tap your screen in a given time period. It's a popular way to test your clicking speed and accuracy, especially among gamers who need quick reflexes for competitive gaming. The test works by counting the number of clicks you make within a specified timeframe and calculating your average clicks per second. Factors like mouse quality, clicking technique, and practice can significantly influence your results. Many gamers use CPS tests to improve their reaction time and clicking accuracy.

How many clicks per second is fast?

Less than 5 CPS: speed of the turtle.
5 to 10 CPS: A good result.
More than 10 CPS: faster than most players.

How can I improve my clicking speed?

  • Use different mouse click techniques such as jitter click or butterfly click.
  • Try to improve the mouse sensitivity in the computer settings.
  • Use a gaming mouse instead of a regular one.
  • Practice mouse speed more often, for example when playing video games.
